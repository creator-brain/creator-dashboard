How the data moves

  1. Daily cron, 06:00 SGT (not set up yet)A scheduled job will read new Gmail threads, WhatsApp groups and Instagram DMs since the last run.
  2. Extract and matchEach conversation becomes a deal. It matches existing rows by Gmail Thread URL, then by Brand + Agency, so reruns update instead of duplicating.
  3. Upsert into NotionFills Status, Next Step, Deadline, Fee and Last Activity. Notion stays the single source of truth.
  4. This board reads NotionOn load and when you press Refresh, it reads every row through your Notion connector, so edits made in Notion show up here.
  5. Your edits write backDragging a card or editing a field sends a property update to that Notion page straight away and stamps Last Activity.
